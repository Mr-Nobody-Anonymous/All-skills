## 🎯 Skill Entry Standard Format

Every skill in the platform conforms to the canonical [**SKILL_ENTRY.template.md**](../templates/SKILL_ENTRY.template.md):
1. **Description**: What the skill is and its architectural significance.
2. **Prerequisites**: Foundation requirements prior to activation.
3. **Learning Path**: 4-stage progression (Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced → Expert).
4. **Resources**: Books, Video Courses, Articles, Certifications, and Communities.
5. **Tools**: Runtimes, compilers, profilers, and frameworks.
6. **Related Skills**: Interconnected graph dependencies.
7. **Industry Demand**: Career roles, salary relevance, and market applications.
8. **Practice Projects**: Concrete beginner, intermediate, and advanced portfolio builds.
