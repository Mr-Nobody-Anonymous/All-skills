# 🎙️ Top Engineering & Technology Podcasts

---

## Frontier AI & Machine Learning
- **Latent Space**: The AI Engineer Podcast (Alessio Fanelli & Swyx)
- **No Priors**: AI, frontier research, and venture investing (Sarah Guo & Elad Gil)
- **Machine Learning Street Talk (MLST)**: Academic discussions on AGI, mathematics, and cognitive science.
- **The Robot Brains Podcast**: Robotics, autonomous systems, and neural networks.

## Software Architecture & Systems
- **Software Engineering Daily**: Deep architectural interviews with production engineers.
- **The Changelog**: Software development, open-source maintainers, and tech culture.
- **CoRecursive**: The human stories and intense debugging adventures behind code.
- **O'Reilly Radar**: Emerging technology trends and enterprise adoption.

## Cybersecurity
- **Darknet Diaries**: Captivating true stories from the dark side of the internet.
- **Risky Business**: The premier weekly cybersecurity and intelligence news digest.
- **Smashing Security**: Humorous and insightful cybersecurity discussions.
