# 💬 Developer Communities & Forums

---

## Discussion Forums & Aggregators
- **Hacker News (news.ycombinator.com)**: Premier technology, engineering, and startup discussion.
- **Lobsters (lobste.rs)**: Community-focused computing and engineering discussions.
- **Stack Overflow / Stack Exchange**: Specific algorithmic and programming debugging.

## Active Subreddits
- **r/programming**: General programming discussion and articles.
- **r/LocalLLaMA**: Open-source local AI models, quantization, and fine-tuning.
- **r/MachineLearning**: Academic research papers and ML industry analysis.
- **r/netsec**: Applied network security, CVE disclosures, and threat research.
- **r/devops & r/kubernetes**: Cloud native workflows and cluster administration.

## Developer Discord Servers
- **The AI Engineer Discord**: Multi-agent systems, RAG, and frontier prompting.
- **Together AI / Hugging Face**: Open-source models and community fine-tuning.
- **Rust Programming Language Community Discord**: Systems programming collaboration.
- **Kubernetes Community Slack**: Direct interaction with SIG maintainers.
