# 📖 Authoritative Books by Category

A curated selection of foundational and advanced textbooks across core computing and engineering disciplines.

---

## 1. Computer Science Fundamentals & Algorithms
- **Introduction to Algorithms (CLRS)** — *Cormen, Leiserson, Rivest, Stein*
- **Algorithms** — *Robert Sedgewick & Kevin Wayne*
- **The Art of Computer Programming** — *Donald Knuth*
- **Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs (SICP)** — *Abelson & Sussman*

## 2. Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
- **Deep Learning** — *Ian Goodfellow, Yoshua Bengio, Aaron Courville*
- **Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras, and TensorFlow** — *Aurélien Géron*
- **Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning** — *Christopher Bishop*
- **Designing Machine Learning Systems** — *Chip Huyen*

## 3. Systems, Architecture & Distributed Systems
- **Designing Data-Intensive Applications (DDIA)** — *Martin Kleppmann*
- **Computer Systems: A Programmer's Perspective (CS:APP)** — *Bryant & O'Hallaron*
- **Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces (OSTEP)** — *Arpaci-Dusseau*
- **Site Reliability Engineering** — *Betsy Beyer, Chris Jones, Jennifer Petoff (Google)*

## 4. Software Craftsmanship & Design Patterns
- **Design Patterns: Elements of Reusable Object-Oriented Software** — *Gang of Four (GoF)*
- **Clean Code & The Clean Coder** — *Robert C. Martin*
- **Refactoring: Improving the Design of Existing Code** — *Martin Fowler*
- **A Philosophy of Software Design** — *John Ousterhout*

## 5. Cybersecurity & Networking
- **The Web Application Hacker's Handbook** — *Dafydd Stuttard & Marcus Pinto*
- **TCP/IP Illustrated (Volume 1)** — *W. Richard Stevens*
- **Practical Malware Analysis** — *Michael Sikorski & Andrew Honig*
- **Serious Cryptography** — *Jean-Philippe Aumasson*
