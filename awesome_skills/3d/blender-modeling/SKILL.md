# 3D Modeling & Blender Workflows

## Scope
Poly-modeling, subdivision surface workflows, UV unwrapping, procedural texturing, and realtime asset optimization in Blender.

## Modeling Workflows
- **Subdivision Surface Modeling**: Maintaining quad-dominant topology, edge loops, and holding/crease edges for clean curvature subdivision.
- **UV Unwrapping**: Minimizing texture distortion; placing UV seams along natural part crevices; packing UV islands with consistent Texel Density ($px/m$).
- **Physically Based Rendering (PBR)**: Principled BSDF shader using Albedo, Metallic, Roughness, Normal (tangent space), and Ambient Occlusion maps.

## Tools & Standards
- **Software**: Blender 4.x, Substance 3D Painter, Marmoset Toolbag.
- **Standards**: glTF 2.0 (OpenGL Transmission Format), USD (Universal Scene Description).
