# Trigonometry

## Scope
Trigonometry examines relationships between side lengths and angles of triangles, circular functions, harmonic oscillations, and spherical coordinate transformations.

## Core Formulations
- **Pythagorean Identities**: $\sin^2 \theta + \cos^2 \theta = 1$, $1 + \tan^2 \theta = \sec^2 \theta$.
- **Angle Addition & Half-Angle**:
  $$\sin(\alpha \pm \beta) = \sin \alpha \cos \beta \pm \cos \alpha \sin \beta, \quad \cos(\alpha \pm \beta) = \cos \alpha \cos \beta \mp \sin \alpha \sin \beta$$
- **Law of Sines & Law of Cosines**:
  $$\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C}, \quad c^2 = a^2 + b^2 - 2ab \cos C$$
- **Spherical Law of Cosines**: $\cos c = \cos a \cos b + \sin a \sin b \cos C$.
