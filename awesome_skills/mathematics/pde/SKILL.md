# Partial Differential Equations (PDE)

## Scope
Partial differential equations govern physical fields across multidimensional space and time, categorized into elliptic, parabolic, and hyperbolic classes.

## Canonical Classes & Methods
- **Elliptic (Laplace / Poisson)**: $\nabla^2 u = f$, describes steady-state potentials, minimum/maximum principles.
- **Parabolic (Heat / Diffusion)**: $\frac{\partial u}{\partial t} = \alpha \nabla^2 u$, maximum principle, smoothing of initial conditions.
- **Hyperbolic (Wave Equation)**: $\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial t^2} = c^2 \nabla^2 u$, finite propagation speed, d'Alembert's solution.
- **Methods**: Separation of variables, Fourier transform, Green's functions, Finite Element Method (FEM).
