# Probability

## Scope
Probability theory provides mathematical models for random phenomena, uncertainty quantification, and stochastic processes.

## Axiomatic Foundations
- **Kolmogorov Axioms**: $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, P)$, $P(\Omega) = 1$, countable additivity for disjoint events.
- **Bayes' Theorem**:
  $$P(A|B) = \frac{P(B|A) P(A)}{P(B)}$$
- **Law of Large Numbers (LLN)**: Sample average $\bar{X}_n \to \mu$ as $n \to \infty$ almost surely.
- **Central Limit Theorem (CLT)**:
  $$\sqrt{n}\left(\frac{\bar{X}_n - \mu}{\sigma}\right) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$$

## Tools & References
- **Canonical References**: Feller — *An Introduction to Probability Theory and Its Applications*; Grimmett & Stirzaker.
