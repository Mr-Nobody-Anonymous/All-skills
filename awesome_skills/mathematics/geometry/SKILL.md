# Geometry

## Scope
Geometry concerns questions of shape, size, relative position of figures, and the properties of space across Euclidean, hyperbolic, elliptic, and projective manifolds.

## Core Principles
- **Euclidean Axioms & Non-Euclidean Variants**: Playfair's parallel postulate; Hyperbolic geometry (infinite parallels, triangle angle sum $<180^\circ$), Elliptic/Spherical geometry (no parallels, angle sum $>180^\circ$).
- **Differential Geometry**: Metric tensor $g_{ij}$, Christoffel symbols $\Gamma_{jk}^i$, Riemann curvature tensor $R^l_{ijk}$, Gauss-Bonnet theorem: $\int_M K dA + \int_{\partial M} k_g ds = 2\pi \chi(M)$.

## Tools & References
- **Software**: GeoGebra, SageMath (Manifolds).
- **Canonical References**: do Carmo — *Differential Geometry of Curves and Surfaces*; Coxeter — *Introduction to Geometry*.
