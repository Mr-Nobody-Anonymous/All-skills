# Graph Theory

## Scope
Graph theory investigates mathematical structures made of vertices connected by edges, modeling networks in computer science, biology, transport, and physics.

## Core Theorems & Formulations
- **Handshaking Lemma**: $\sum_{v \in V} \deg(v) = 2 |E|$.
- **Eulerian Graphs**: Connected graph has closed Eulerian trail iff every vertex has even degree.
- **Euler's Formula for Planar Graphs**: $V - E + F = 2$.
- **Max-Flow Min-Cut Theorem** (Ford-Fulkerson): Maximum network flow equals the capacity of the minimum cut.
- **Spectral Graph Theory**: Graph Laplacian $L = D - A$, eigenvalues characterize connectivity (algebraic connectivity $\lambda_2$, Cheeger's inequality).
