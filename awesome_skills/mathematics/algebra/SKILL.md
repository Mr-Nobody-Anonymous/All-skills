# Algebra

## Scope
Algebra studies mathematical symbols and the rules for manipulating these symbols in formulas; it covers equations, polynomials, rational functions, inequalities, and structural relations.

## Core Formulations
- **Fundamental Theorem of Algebra**: Every non-zero single-variable polynomial of degree $n$ over $\mathbb{C}$ has exactly $n$ roots counting multiplicity.
- **Quadratic & Higher Polynomials**:
  - Quadratic formula: $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$
  - Vieta's formulas: For $P(x) = a_n x^n + \dots + a_0$, $\sum r_i = -a_{n-1}/a_n$, $\prod r_i = (-1)^n a_0/a_n$.
- **Rational Functions & Partial Fractions**: Decomposing $P(x)/Q(x)$ into simpler fractions for integration and analysis.
