# Mathematical Modeling

## Scope
Mathematical modeling formulates mathematical representations of physical, biological, engineering, and socioeconomic systems to predict behavior and optimize performance.

## Systematic Modeling Protocol
1. **System Definition & Assumptions**: State explicit simplifications and boundary conditions.
2. **Dimensional Analysis (Buckingham $\pi$ Theorem)**: An equation of $n$ physical variables involving $k$ independent fundamental dimensions can be written in terms of $p = n - k$ dimensionless parameters.
3. **Model Selection**: Mechanistic differential equations vs. empirical/statistical vs. discrete/agent-based.
4. **Calibration & Sensitivity Analysis**: Global sensitivity analysis (Sobol indices), parameter estimation.
5. **Validation**: Test predictions on independent held-out data.

## Tools & References
- **Software**: MATLAB/Simulink, Modelica, SimBiology, Python (`scipy`).
- **Canonical References**: Meerschaert — *Mathematical Modeling*; Giordano, Fox & Horton.
