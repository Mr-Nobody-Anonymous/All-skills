# Functional Capacity & Occupational Profile Analysis

Occupational therapy practice framework (OTPF-4), Canadian Occupational Performance Measure (COPM), range of motion (goniometry), and manual muscle testing (MMT).

## Standard Operating Procedure & Methodology

### 1. Requirements Formulation & Clinical / Professional Guidelines
Establish the foundational evidence-based principles for functional capacity & occupational profile analysis:
- Review governing guidelines, international standards (e.g. WHO, CDC, CLSI, ANSI, HL7, FHIR, ISO), and regulatory mandates.
- Establish verified baseline criteria, diagnostic metrics, and operational boundaries.
- Formulate standardized intake, assessment, or processing workflows.

### 2. Operational & Analytical Execution Steps
1. **Intake & Verification**: Capture and validate all required source data, signals, or patient/clinical/enterprise context.
2. **Standardized Evaluation**: Execute validated diagnostic, therapeutic, communicative, or retrieval algorithms per established professional protocols.
3. **Quality & Risk Control**: Verify thresholds, check for anomalies, and confirm compliance with ethical, clinical, or communicative standards.
4. **Documentation & Audit Trail**: Structure all findings, actionable recommendations, and referral/escalation pathways into a machine-readable, verifiable report.

### 3. Verification & Compliance Contract
Every execution must verify:
- Complete compliance with established domain standards and absence of unverified assumptions.
- Rigorous adherence to privacy, safety, and evidence-based clinical or communicative best practices.
- Clear, auditable documentation suitable for peer review, clinical record, or enterprise deployment.

## Reference Architecture & Standards
Applies validated reference architectures, objective scoring matrices, and standard operating procedures to ensure repeatable, high-reliability outcomes across professional and agentic environments.
