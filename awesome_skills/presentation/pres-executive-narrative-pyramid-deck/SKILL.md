# Executive Presentation Architecture and Minto Pyramid Storyboarding

## Overview & Core Principles
Structure high-impact board and executive presentations using the Barbara Minto Pyramid Principle and SCQA (Situation-Complication-Question-Answer) frameworks.

### Minto Pyramid Structure & SCQA Invariants
1. **SCQA Narrative Introduction**:
   - Situation: Undisputed, baseline context familiar to all stakeholders.
   - Complication: External disruption, constraint, or threat perturbing the situation.
   - Question: "How do we adapt / capitalize / remediate?"
   - Answer: The governing thesis statement and recommended strategic decision.
2. **Top-Down Pyramid Hierarchy**:
   - Governing Thought (The Headline Recommendation).
   - Supporting Arguments (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive - MECE).
   - Empirical Evidence & Financial Justification.
3. **Slide Design Invariants**:
   - Action Titles: Every slide header must be a complete declarative takeaway sentence, not a category label (e.g., "Migrating to Multi-Region Reduces Latency by 42%", not "Performance Overview").
