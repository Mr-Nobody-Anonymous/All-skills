# Vocal Projection, Body Language, and Stage Presence Mastery

## Overview & Core Principles
Master diaphragmatic breathing, vocal variety, eye contact triangulation, and purposeful stage movement.

Eliminate filler words (um, ah, like) through strategic pauses and project voice using chest resonance.
