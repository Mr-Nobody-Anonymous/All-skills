# Smart City IoT Systems

## Scope
Smart city engineering deploys citywide sensor and communication networks to optimize municipal infrastructure, traffic flows, public utilities, and environmental resilience.

## Urban Application Verticals
- **Intelligent Street Lighting**: DALI/0-10V dimming controllers reporting power consumption and bulb failure over cellular NB-IoT or LoRaWAN.
- **Smart Water Metering (AMR/AMI)**: Acoustic leak detection and ultrasonic water consumption telemetry transmitting on sub-GHz ISM bands.
- **Environmental & Air Quality Monitoring**: Particulate matter ($PM_{2.5}, PM_{10}$), $NO_2$, and sound level telemetry mapping urban microclimates.

## Tools & Standards
- **Standards**: ITU-T Y.4900 (Key performance indicators for smart sustainable cities), LoRaWAN.
