# Connected Vehicles & Telematics

## Scope
Vehicle telematics connects automotive electronic control units (ECUs) to fleet management platforms and intelligent transportation systems via cellular and V2X wireless links.

## Automotive Communication Protocols
- **OBD-II (SAE J1979)**: Diagnostics over CAN; Standard PIDs for vehicle speed, engine RPM, coolant temperature, fuel trim, and DTC fault codes.
- **Telematics Control Unit (TCU)**: Embedded unit combining GPS/GNSS receiver, 4G/5G modem, CAN transceiver, and inertial sensors (crash detection).
- **V2X Communication**: Cellular V2X (C-V2X, 3GPP Release 14/15/16) and DSRC (IEEE 802.11p) for Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) collision avoidance and Vehicle-to-Infrastructure (V2I) traffic priority.

## Tools & Standards
- **Standards**: SAE J1939 (Heavy-duty vehicles), SAE J1979 (OBD-II), ISO 14229 (UDS).
