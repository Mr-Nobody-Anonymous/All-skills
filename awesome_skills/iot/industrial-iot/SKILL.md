# Industrial IoT (IIoT) & Industry 4.0

## Scope
Industrial IoT connects factory machinery, PLCs, sensors, and SCADA systems to enterprise analytics platforms while respecting real-time industrial safety constraints.

## Industrial Protocols & ISA-95
- **Purdue Model / ISA-95 Hierarchy**:
  - Level 0: Physical production process.
  - Level 1: Sensors & Actuators.
  - Level 2: Control Systems (PLCs, DCS).
  - Level 3: Manufacturing Operations Management (MES, SCADA).
  - Level 4: Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP).
- **Protocols**:
  - Modbus (RTU over RS-485, TCP over Ethernet): Register-based polling (Holding Registers, Input Registers).
  - OPC Unified Architecture (OPC-UA, IEC 62541): Secure, cross-platform, object-oriented semantic information model.

## Tools & Standards
- **Standards**: IEC 62541 (OPC UA), ISA-95.
- **Software**: Node-RED, Ignition SCADA, open62541, Kepware.
