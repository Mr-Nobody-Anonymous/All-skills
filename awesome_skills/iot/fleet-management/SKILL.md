# Fleet Management Telematics

## Scope
Fleet management telemetry tracks, monitors, and optimizes commercial vehicular assets, ensuring regulatory compliance, fuel efficiency, and cold-chain cargo integrity.

## Fleet Systems & Analytics
- **GNSS Tracking & Geofencing**: Real-time position tracking, ray-casting polygon geofence breach detection, dead-reckoning during GPS tunnel outages.
- **Cold Chain Monitoring**: Continuous multi-zone temperature and humidity logging with automated alert dispatching upon threshold breach.
- **Regulatory Compliance**: Electronic Logging Devices (ELD, FMCSA mandate) recording Hours of Service (HOS).

## Standards & References
- **Standards**: FMCSA Part 395 (Electronic Logging Devices).
