# Smart Home Automation

## Scope
Smart home engineering integrates environmental controls, smart lighting, security systems, access controls, and home appliances into cohesive, privacy-preserving automated ecosystems.

## System Topology & Integration
- **Local vs. Cloud Control**: Local-first processing (Home Assistant, Hubitat) ensuring automations execute with sub-second latency even during Internet outages.
- **Hub Architecture**: Multiprotocol transceivers bridging Zigbee, Z-Wave, Thread, and Wi-Fi devices into unified state engines.
- **Automation Logic**: Event-Trigger-Condition-Action (ETCA) pipelines for energy optimization, presence simulation, and climate setback schedules.

## Tools & Standards
- **Software**: Home Assistant, openHAB, Node-RED.
- **Standards**: Matter, Zigbee, Z-Wave Plus.
