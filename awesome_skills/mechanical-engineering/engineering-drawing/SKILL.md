# Engineering Drawing & GD&T

## Scope
Engineering drawing communicates physical part specifications, geometric boundaries, assembly fits, and quality control parameters through standardized 2D/3D technical drawings.

## GD&T Standards (ASME Y14.5-2018)
- **Feature Control Frame**: `[Geometric Symbol | Tolerance Zone | Material Modifier | Primary Datum | Secondary | Tertiary]`.
- **14 Geometric Characteristics**:
  - Form: Straightness, Flatness, Circularity, Cylindricity (no datums).
  - Orientation: Perpendicularity, Parallelism, Angularity.
  - Location: Position (true position tolerance zone: $\varnothing 2\sqrt{\Delta x^2 + \Delta y^2}$), Concentricity, Symmetry.
  - Runout: Circular Runout, Total Runout.
  - Profile: Profile of a Surface, Profile of a Line.
- **Material Condition Modifiers**: Maximum Material Condition (MMC, $\textcircled{M}$), Least Material Condition (LMC, $\textcircled{L}$), Regardless of Feature Size (RFS).
- **Fit Classes (ISO 286 / ANSI B4.1)**: Clearance fit (RC/H7-g6), Transition fit (LOC/H7-k6), Interference/Press fit (FN/H7-s6).
