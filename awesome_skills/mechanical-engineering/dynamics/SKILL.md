# Mechanical Dynamics

## Scope
Mechanical dynamics explores the motion of accelerated bodies and mechanical systems under the action of applied forces and moments.

## Formulations & Methods
- **Planar Rigid Body Kinematics**:
  $$\mathbf{v}_B = \mathbf{v}_A + \mathbf{\omega} \times \mathbf{r}_{B/A}, \quad \mathbf{a}_B = \mathbf{a}_A + \mathbf{\alpha} \times \mathbf{r}_{B/A} - \omega^2 \mathbf{r}_{B/A}$$
- **Newton-Euler Equations of Motion**:
  $$\sum \mathbf{F} = m \mathbf{a}_G, \quad \sum \mathbf{M}_G = \mathbf{I}_G \mathbf{\alpha} + \mathbf{\omega} \times (\mathbf{I}_G \mathbf{\omega})$$
- **Parallel Axis Theorem**: $I = I_G + m d^2$.
- **Gyroscopic Precession**: $\mathbf{M} = \mathbf{\Omega}_p \times \mathbf{H} = \mathbf{\Omega}_p \times (I_s \omega_s \mathbf{k})$.
