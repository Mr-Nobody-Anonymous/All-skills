# Computer-Aided Design (CAD)

## Scope
Computer-aided design creates parametric 3D models, complex assemblies, kinematic linkages, sheet metal flats, and digital manufacturing definitions (STEP AP242).

## Parametric Modeling Invariants
- **Sketch Constraints**: Fully constrained sketches (geometric coincidences, collinearity, tangency) prior to feature generation (extrude, revolve, sweep, loft).
- **Top-Down vs. Bottom-Up Assembly**:
  - Bottom-Up: Mate pre-modeled components.
  - Top-Down: Skeleton modeling and in-context references for automated propagating updates.
- **Model-Based Definition (MBD / 3D Annotations)**: Embedding GD&T and product manufacturing information (PMI) directly into native 3D geometry (ASME Y14.41).

## Tools & Standards
- **Software**: SolidWorks, Autodesk Fusion 360, PTC Creo, Siemens NX, CATIA, FreeCAD.
- **Standards**: ISO 10303 (STEP AP214 / AP242), ASME Y14.41.
