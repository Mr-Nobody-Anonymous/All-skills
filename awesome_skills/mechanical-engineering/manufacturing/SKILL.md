# Manufacturing Processes & DFMA

## Scope
Manufacturing engineering transforms raw materials into finished components through casting, bulk deformation, sheet metal forming, polymer processing, and assembly.

## Manufacturing Regimes & Design Rules
- **Design for Manufacturing & Assembly (DFMA)**:
  - Minimize part count (combine compliant mechanisms).
  - Provide self-aligning features (chamfers, dowel pins).
  - Standardize fasteners across assemblies.
- **Injection Molding Principles**:
  - Uniform wall thickness to avoid sink marks and differential shrinkage.
  - Draft angles: $1^\circ - 2^\circ$ minimum per side for core/cavity release.
  - Rib thickness: $50-60\%$ of nominal adjacent wall thickness.
- **Sheet Metal Forming**: Minimum bend radius $R \ge t$; Bend allowance: $BA = \frac{\pi}{180} \theta (R + K t)$ where $K$ is K-factor ($0.33-0.50$).
