# Maintenance Engineering

## Scope
Maintenance engineering ensures equipment reliability, operational readiness, and asset longevity through preventive, predictive, and condition-based monitoring programs.

## Strategies & Analysis
- **Reliability-Centered Maintenance (RCM, SAE JA1011)**: Identifies failure modes and consequence categories (safety, environmental, operational, non-operational).
- **Predictive Maintenance (PdM) Diagnostics**:
  - Vibration Analysis: FFT spectra; unbalance (1X RPM), misalignment (2X RPM), bearing fault frequencies (BPFI, BPFO, BSF, FTF).
  - Infrared Thermography: Electrical hotspot detection, bearing overheating.
  - Oil Analysis: Particle count (ISO 4406), viscosity, moisture, elemental spectroscopy.
- **Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE)**:
  $$\text{OEE} = \text{Availability} \times \text{Performance} \times \text{Quality}$$

## Tools & Standards
- **Standards**: ISO 55000 (Asset Management), SAE JA1011 (RCM).
- **Software**: IBM Maximo, SAP PM, UpKeep, Fiix.
