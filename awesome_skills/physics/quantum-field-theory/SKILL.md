# Quantum Field Theory (QFT)

## Scope
Quantum field theory unites quantum mechanics, special relativity, and classical field theory to describe subatomic particles as excitations of underlying fields.

## Core Formalisms
- **Second Quantization**: Field operators $\psi(x)$ satisfying canonical commutation (bosons) or anti-commutation (fermions) relations: $\{\psi(x), \psi^\dagger(y)\} = \delta^3(x - y)$.
- **Klein-Gordon (Spin 0)**: $(\partial_\mu \partial^\mu + m^2)\phi = 0$.
- **Dirac Equation (Spin 1/2)**: $(i \gamma^\mu \partial_\mu - m)\psi = 0$.
- **Path Integral Formulation**:
  $$\mathcal{Z}[J] = \int \mathcal{D}\phi \exp\left(i \int d^4x [\mathcal{L}(\phi) + J(x)\phi(x)]\right)$$
- **Feynman Diagrams & Renormalization**: Perturbation series expansion, loop integrals, dimensional regularization, running coupling constants ($\beta$-function).

## Tools & References
- **Canonical References**: Peskin & Schroeder — *An Introduction to Quantum Field Theory*; Weinberg — *The Quantum Theory of Fields*.
