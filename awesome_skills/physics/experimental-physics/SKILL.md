# Experimental Physics

## Scope
Experimental physics designs, executes, and analyzes physical experiments, managing laboratory instrumentation, vacuum systems, cryogenics, and signal-to-noise optimization.

## Experimental Systems & Formulations
- **Error Propagation**: For $q = f(x_1, \dots, x_n)$:
  $$\delta q = \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^n \left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_i}\right)^2 (\delta x_i)^2}$$
- **Vacuum Physics**: Knudsen number $Kn = \lambda / d$. Regimes: Viscous flow ($Kn < 0.01$), Molecular flow ($Kn > 1$). Turbomolecular, ion, and cryogenic pumping down to UHV ($<10^{-9}\text{ mbar}$).
- **Signal-to-Noise Enhancement**: Lock-in amplification (phase-sensitive detection), Johnson-Nyquist noise $v_n = \sqrt{4 k_B T R \Delta f}$.
