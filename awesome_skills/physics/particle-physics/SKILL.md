# Particle Physics

## Scope
Particle physics studies the fundamental constituents of matter and radiation and the fundamental forces governing their interactions (Standard Model).

## Standard Model Framework
- **Fermions (Matter, Spin 1/2)**:
  - Quarks: $(u, d)$, $(c, s)$, $(t, b)$ (carry color charge: red, green, blue).
  - Leptons: $(e, \nu_e)$, $(\mu, \nu_\mu)$, $(\tau, \nu_\tau)$ (color singlets).
- **Gauge Bosons (Forces, Spin 1)**:
  - Gluons ($8$ generators of $SU(3)_C$, Strong force).
  - $W^\pm, Z^0$ ($SU(2)_L \times U(1)_Y$, Weak force).
  - Photon $\gamma$ ($U(1)_{EM}$, Electromagnetism).
- **Scalar Boson (Spin 0)**: Higgs boson ($125\text{ GeV}$), gives mass via spontaneous symmetry breaking (Higgs mechanism).
