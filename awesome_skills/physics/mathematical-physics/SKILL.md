# Mathematical Physics

## Scope
Mathematical physics develops rigorous mathematical methods and theoretical structures for solving problems in physics.

## Core Mathematical Tools
- **Hilbert Spaces & Spectral Theory**: Self-adjoint operators on $L^2(\mathbb{R}^n)$, resolution of the identity.
- **Lie Groups & Lie Algebras**: $SO(3)$, $SU(2)$, $SO(3,1)$ (Lorentz group), commutation relations $[J_i, J_j] = i \epsilon_{ijk} J_k$.
- **Distribution Theory**: Dirac delta $\delta(x)$, generalized functions, Sobolev spaces $H^s(\mathbb{R}^n)$.
- **Green's Functions**: Solutions to linear differential operators $\mathcal{L} G(x, x') = \delta(x - x')$.

## Tools & References
- **Canonical References**: Arfken, Weber & Harris — *Mathematical Methods for Physicists*; Courant & Hilbert.
