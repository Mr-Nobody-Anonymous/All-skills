# Nuclear Physics

## Scope
Nuclear physics explores the structure, reactions, and decay of atomic nuclei, nuclear forces, fission, and stellar nucleosynthesis.

## Core Nuclear Formulations
- **Semi-Empirical Mass Formula (Bethe-Weizsäcker Liquid Drop)**:
  $$B(A, Z) = a_v A - a_s A^{2/3} - a_c \frac{Z(Z-1)}{A^{1/3}} - a_a \frac{(A - 2Z)^2}{A} \pm \delta(A, Z)$$
- **Nuclear Shell Model**: Magic numbers (2, 8, 20, 28, 50, 82, 126) explained by strong spin-orbit coupling ($l \cdot s$).
- **Radioactive Decay Law**: $N(t) = N_0 e^{-\lambda t}$, activity $A = \lambda N$ (Bq).
- **Cross Section ($\sigma$) & Reaction Rate**: $R = n_t \phi \sigma$, where $\phi$ is neutron flux ($n/cm^2/s$).
