# Paleontology

## Scope
Paleontology studies the history of life on Earth through the fossil record, organism evolution, paleoecological communities, and biostratigraphic correlation.

## Core Methodologies
- **Taphonomy**: Processes of fossilization: permineralization, carbonization, mold/cast, recrystallization.
- **Biostratigraphy**: Biozones (interval zones, assemblage zones, abundance zones); Index fossils (rapid evolution, broad geographic distribution, easily identifiable).
- **Big Five Mass Extinctions**: End-Ordovician, Late Devonian, End-Permian (P-Tr, largest), End-Triassic, End-Cretaceous (K-Pg, bolide impact).
