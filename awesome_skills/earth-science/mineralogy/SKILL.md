# Mineralogy

## Scope
Mineralogy studies the chemical composition, crystalline structure, physical properties, and geological occurrence of minerals.

## Crystallography & Silicate Classes

### 1. Crystal Systems & Symmetry
- 7 Crystal Systems: Triclinic, Monoclinic, Orthorhombic, Tetragonal, Trigonal, Hexagonal, Isometric (Cubic).
- 32 Point Groups and 230 Space Groups.
- **Bragg's Law**: $n\lambda = 2d_{hkl} \sin \theta$.

### 2. Silicate Classifications ($[SiO_4]^{4-}$ Tetrahedron)
- **Nesosilicates** (isolated tetrahedra): Olivine, Garnet ($[SiO_4]$).
- **Sorosilicates** (paired): Epidote ($[Si_2O_7]$).
- **Inosilicates** (chains): Pyroxenes (single chain, $[Si_2O_6]$), Amphiboles (double chain, $[Si_8O_{22}]$).
- **Phyllosilicates** (sheets): Micas (biotite, muscovite), Clays, Serpentine ($[Si_2O_5]$).
- **Tectosilicates** (3D framework): Quartz ($SiO_2$), Feldspars (Orthoclase, Plagioclase).
