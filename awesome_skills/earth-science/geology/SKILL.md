# Geology

## Scope
Geology investigates the Earth's composition, structure, physical properties, history, and the geological processes that shape its crust and interior over deep time.

## Core Geological Frameworks

### 1. The Rock Cycle & Petrogenesis
- **Igneous**: Formed by magma/lava solidification.
  - Intrusive (plutonic): Slow cooling, coarse phaneritic texture (granite, gabbro).
  - Extrusive (volcanic): Rapid cooling, aphanitic/glassy texture (basalt, rhyolite, obsidian).
  - Bowen's Reaction Series: Continuous (plagioclase Ca $\to$ Na) and Discontinuous (olivine $\to$ pyroxene $\to$ amphibole $\to$ biotite).
- **Sedimentary**: Formed by weathering, erosion, deposition, compaction, and cementation (lithification).
  - Clastic: Conglomerate, sandstone, shale (Wentworth scale).
  - Chemical/Biogenic: Limestone ($CaCO_3$), chert ($SiO_2$), evaporites (halite, gypsum).
- **Metamorphic**: Solid-state alteration by heat, pressure, and chemically active fluids.
  - Foliated: Slate $\to$ phyllite $\to$ schist $\to$ gneiss (increasing metamorphic grade).
  - Non-foliated: Marble (from limestone), quartzite (from sandstone).

### 2. Stratigraphy & Geologic Time
- **Steno's Principles**: Superposition, Original Horizontality, Lateral Continuity, Cross-Cutting Relationships.
- **Unconformities**: Angular unconformity, disconformity, nonconformity.
- **Radiometric Dating**: $N(t) = N_0 e^{-\lambda t}$, $t_{1/2} = \ln 2 / \lambda$ ($^{40}K-^{40}Ar$, $^{238}U-^{206}Pb$, $^{14}C$).
