# Petrology

## Scope
Petrology investigates the origin, occurrence, structure, and history of rocks through field relations, petrographic microscopy, and geochemical thermodynamics.

## Petrographic Principles & Phase Equilibria
- **Phase Rule (Gibbs)**: $F = C - P + 2$ (or $F = C - P + 1$ at constant pressure).
- **Binary & Ternary Eutectic Systems**: Diopside-Anorthite, Albite-Anorthite (solid solution), Qtz-Ab-Or (granite system).
- **Metamorphic Facies**: Zeolite $\to$ Greenschist $\to$ Amphibolite $\to$ Granulite (normal geothermal gradient); Blueschist $\to$ Eclogite (high P, low T subduction zones).
