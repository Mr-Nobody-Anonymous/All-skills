# Hydrology

## Scope
Hydrology examines the movement, distribution, and management of water across the Earth, covering precipitation, evapotranspiration, surface runoff, infiltration, and watershed dynamics.

## Hydrologic Formulations
- **Water Balance Equation**:
  $$P - ET - Q - G = \frac{\Delta S}{\Delta t}$$
- **Rational Method (Peak Discharge)**: $Q_p = C \cdot I \cdot A$.
- **Infiltration (Horton's Equation)**:
  $$f(t) = f_c + (f_0 - f_c) e^{-kt}$$
- **Unit Hydrograph Theory**: Direct runoff resulting from 1 unit of effective rainfall uniformly distributed over a watershed.
