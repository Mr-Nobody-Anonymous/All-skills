# Geomorphology

## Scope
Geomorphology studies landforms, their origin, evolution, and physical/chemical processes acting on the Earth's surface.

## Process Domains
- **Hillslope**: Mass wasting (creep, landslides, debris flows), Coulomb failure criterion.
- **Fluvial**: Stream power equation $\Omega = \rho g Q S$, hydraulic geometry, sediment transport (Shields parameter).
- **Glacial**: Glacial abrasion and plucking, U-shaped valleys, moraines, cirques.
- **Landscape Evolution Models (LEMs)**: Stream power incision law: $\partial z / \partial t = U - K A^m S^n$.
