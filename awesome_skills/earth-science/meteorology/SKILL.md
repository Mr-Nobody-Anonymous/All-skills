# Meteorology

## Scope
Meteorology studies the atmosphere, atmospheric phenomena, weather forecasting, and atmospheric physics/dynamics across micro, meso, and synoptic scales.

## Atmospheric Dynamics
- **Geostrophic Wind**: Balance between Pressure Gradient Force (PGF) and Coriolis force:
  $$v_g = \frac{1}{\rho f} \frac{\partial P}{\partial x}, \quad u_g = -\frac{1}{\rho f} \frac{\partial P}{\partial y}$$
  where $f = 2\Omega \sin \phi$ is the Coriolis parameter.
- **Hydrostatic Equation**: $\frac{\partial P}{\partial z} = -\rho g$.
- **Hypsometric Equation**: Thickness between pressure levels $z_2 - z_1 = \frac{R_d \bar{T}_v}{g} \ln\left(\frac{P_1}{P_2}\right)$.
