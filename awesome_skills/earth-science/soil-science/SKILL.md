# Soil Science

## Scope
Soil science (pedology and edaphology) studies the formation, classification, physical and chemical properties, and fertility of soils as living natural bodies.

## Core Soil Principles
- **Soil Forming Factors (ClORPT)**: Climate, Organisms, Relief, Parent material, Time.
- **Master Horizons**: O (organic), A (topsoil), E (eluviated), B (subsoil/illuviated), C (parent material), R (bedrock).
- **Soil Water Potential**: $\psi_{\text{total}} = \psi_m (\text{matric}) + \psi_g (\text{gravitational}) + \psi_o (\text{osmotic})$.
- **Cation Exchange Capacity (CEC)**: Retention of exchangeable base cations ($Ca^{2+}, Mg^{2+}, K^+, Na^+$) on clay and organic colloids.

## Standards & References
- **Standards**: USDA Soil Taxonomy, WRB (World Reference Base for Soil Resources).
- **Canonical References**: Brady & Weil — *The Nature and Properties of Soils*.
