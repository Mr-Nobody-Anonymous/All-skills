# Seismology

## Scope
Seismology studies earthquakes, elastic wave propagation through planetary bodies, seismic hazard mitigation, and internal planetary structure.

## Earthquake Physics & Focal Mechanisms
- **Moment Magnitude ($M_w$)**:
  $$M_w = \frac{2}{3} \log_{10}(M_0) - 10.7$$
  where seismic moment $M_0 = \mu A D$ ($\mu$ shear modulus, $A$ fault rupture area, $D$ average slip).
- **Focal Mechanism (Beachballs)**: Stereographic lower-hemisphere projection of first-motion P-wave polarities (compression vs. dilatation); distinguishes normal, reverse (thrust), and strike-slip faulting.
