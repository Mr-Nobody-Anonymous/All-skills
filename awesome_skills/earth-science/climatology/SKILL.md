# Climatology

## Scope
Climatology investigates long-term atmospheric patterns, climate variability, anthropogenic climate change, radiative forcing, and planetary energy balance.

## Climate Dynamics & Forcing
- **Planetary Radiative Equilibrium**:
  $$(1 - \alpha) \frac{S_0}{4} = \sigma T_e^4$$
- **Radiative Forcing of $CO_2$**: $\Delta F = 5.35 \ln(C / C_0)\text{ W/m}^2$.
- **Teleconnections**: El Niño-Southern Oscillation (ENSO, Niño 3.4 index), North Atlantic Oscillation (NAO), Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation (AMOC).

## Standards & References
- **Software**: CDO (Climate Data Operators), NCO, xarray, ESMValTool.
- **Canonical References**: IPCC Sixth Assessment Report (AR6); Hartmann — *Global Physical Climatology*.
