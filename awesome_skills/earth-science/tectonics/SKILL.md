# Tectonics

## Scope
Tectonics studies the regional-to-global architecture, deformation, and evolution of the lithosphere and upper mantle, driven by mantle convection and boundary interactions.

## Plate Boundaries & Kinematics
- **Divergent**: Mid-ocean ridges (MORB volcanism), continental rifts (East African Rift).
- **Convergent**: Subduction zones (oceanic-oceanic island arcs, oceanic-continental volcanic arcs), continental collision (Himalayas).
- **Transform**: Strike-slip plate boundaries (San Andreas Fault).
- **Euler Poles**: Plate rotation on a sphere described by Euler pole coordinates $(\lambda, \phi)$ and angular velocity $\omega$.
