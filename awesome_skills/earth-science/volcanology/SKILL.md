# Volcanology

## Scope
Volcanology examines the generation, ascent, and eruption of magma onto planetary surfaces, volcanic landforms, eruptive hazards, and geodetic/gas monitoring.

## Eruptive Dynamics & Hazards
- **Magma Viscosity**: Controlled by $SiO_2$ content, dissolved volatiles ($H_2O, CO_2, SO_2$), and temperature. Basalt ($50\% SiO_2$, low viscosity) vs. Rhyolite ($70\% SiO_2$, high viscosity).
- **Eruption Styles**: Hawaiian, Strombolian, Vulcanian, Sub-Plinian, Plinian (Volcanic Explosivity Index, VEI 0 to 8).
- **Monitoring Techniques**: InSAR surface deformation, seismic tremor detection, tiltmeters, gas emission spectrometry (COSPEC, DOAS).

## Tools & References
- **Canonical References**: Parfitt & Wilson — *Fundamentals of Volcanology*; Schmincke — *Volcanism*.
