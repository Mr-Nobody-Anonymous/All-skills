# Glaciology

## Scope
Glaciology investigates ice and natural ice phenomena, covering glacier mechanics, ice sheet dynamics, snow metamorphism, and climate interactions.

## Ice Dynamics & Physics
- **Glen's Flow Law** (Viscoplastic ice deformation):
  $$\dot{\varepsilon} = A \tau^n \quad (n \approx 3)$$
- **Shallow Ice Approximation (SIA)**: Basal shear stress $\tau_b = \rho g H \sin \alpha$.
- **Mass Balance**: Accumulation minus ablation ($B = c - a$); Equilibrium Line Altitude (ELA).

## Tools & References
- **Software**: PISM (Parallel Ice Sheet Model), Elmer/Ice.
- **Canonical References**: Cuffey & Paterson — *The Physics of Glaciers* (4th ed.).
