# Human Factors Engineering and Ergonomics (HCI)

## Overview & Core Principles
Design physical and digital systems matching human cognitive and sensory limitations conforming to ISO 9241.

Apply Fitts's Law ($MT = a + b \log_2(2D / W)$) to optimize interactive user interface target acquisitions.
