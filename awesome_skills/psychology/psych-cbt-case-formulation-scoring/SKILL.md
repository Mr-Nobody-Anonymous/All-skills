# Cognitive Behavioral Case Formulation & Psychometric Assessment

## Overview & Core Principles
Execute evidence-based CBT case formulations using Beck's cognitive triad and interpret standardized psychometric instruments (PHQ-9, GAD-7, Big Five).

### Cognitive Behavioral Conceptualization
1. **Beckian Cognitive Architecture**:
   - Core Beliefs (schemas formed in early developmental experience) $\rightarrow$ Intermediate Beliefs (conditional assumptions, rules, attitudes) $\rightarrow$ Automatic Thoughts (involuntary situational appraisals) $\rightarrow$ Emotional, Physiological, and Behavioral Responses.
2. **Cognitive Distortions Classification**:
   - Catastrophizing, Dichotomous Thinking (all-or-nothing), Mind Reading, Emotional Reasoning, Overgeneralization.
3. **Psychometric Scoring & Cutoffs**:
   - PHQ-9 (Depression): 0-4 minimal, 5-9 mild, 10-14 moderate, 15-19 moderately severe, 20-27 severe. Critical check: Item 9 (suicidal ideation).
   - GAD-7 (Anxiety): 0-4 minimal, 5-9 mild, 10-14 moderate, 15-21 severe.
