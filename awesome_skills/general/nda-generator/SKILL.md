# NDA Generator

## Overview
Creates NDA templates. ALWAYS includes legal disclaimer.

## Output includes:
- Mutual or one-way NDA template
- Definition of confidential information
- Exclusions
- Term and duration
- Remedies
- Legal disclaimer

⚠️ Always note: "This template should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before execution."
