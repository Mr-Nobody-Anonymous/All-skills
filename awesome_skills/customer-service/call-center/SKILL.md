# Inbound Call Center Telephony & Queue Architecture

## Overview & Core Principles
Design IVR decision trees, skill-based ACD routing, and monitor average handle time (AHT) and abandon rate.

Enforce 80/20 service level (80% of calls answered within 20 seconds) with dynamic overflow queues.
