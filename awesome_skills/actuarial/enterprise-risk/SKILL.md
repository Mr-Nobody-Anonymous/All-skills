# Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) & Solvency Risk Capital

## Overview & Core Principles
Quantify multi-risk aggregation, correlation matrices, and ORSA (Own Risk and Solvency Assessment) capital allocation.

### Elliptical Copula Aggregation
- Aggregate underwriting, market, credit, and operational capital charges using Cholesky factorized correlation matrices or t-copulas for tail dependence.
