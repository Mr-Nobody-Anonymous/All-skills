# Pension Valuation & Defined Benefit Actuarial Accrual

## Overview & Core Principles
Value Projected Benefit Obligations (PBO) and Accumulated Benefit Obligations (ABO) under IAS 19 and US GAAP ASC 715.

### PBO Actuarial Cost Method
- Projected Unit Credit (PUC) method allocates pension cost proportionally to completed service years: $B_t = \text{Salary}_{\text{final}} \times \text{Accrual Rate} \times t$.
