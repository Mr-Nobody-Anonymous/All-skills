# Catastrophe Modeling & Exceedance Probability Curves

## Overview & Core Principles
Simulate hurricane, flood, and earthquake hazard footprints to construct Occurrence Exceedance Probability (OEP) and Aggregate Exceedance Probability (AEP) curves.

### Exceedance Probability Mechanics
- Return Period $T = 1 / p$, where $p$ is annual exceedance probability.
- Tail Value at Risk (TVaR) / Expected Shortfall at 99th percentile computes tail ruin exposure for reinsurance treaty attachment.
