# Transportation Engineering

## Scope
Transportation engineering designs, plans, and operates facilities for safe, efficient, and resilient movement of people and freight.

## Core Principles & Formulations
- **Traffic Flow Fundamental Equation**: $q = k \cdot v$ (Flow = Density $\times$ Speed).
- **Greenshields Speed-Density Model**: $v = v_f (1 - k / k_j) \implies q_{\max} = \frac{v_f k_j}{4}$.
- **Highway Capacity Manual (HCM) Level of Service (LOS)**: Grades A through F based on volume-to-capacity ($v/c$) ratio, control delay, or density.
- **Four-Step Travel Demand Model**: Trip Generation $\to$ Trip Distribution (Gravity Model) $\to$ Mode Choice (Logit) $\to$ Traffic Assignment.

## Tools & Standards
- **Standards**: AASHTO Green Book (A Policy on Geometric Design of Highways and Streets), TRB Highway Capacity Manual (HCM).
- **Software**: VISSIM, Synchro, TransCAD, Aimsun.
- **Canonical References**: Garber & Hoel — *Traffic and Highway Engineering*.
