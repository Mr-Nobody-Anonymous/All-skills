# Timber Structures

## Scope
Timber structures encompasses the engineering design of sawn lumber, glued-laminated timber (glulam), and mass timber (Cross-Laminated Timber, CLT) under the National Design Specification (NDS).

## NDS Adjustment Factor Methodology
- **Reference Design Value ($F_b, F_t, F_v, F_c$)** adjusted by condition factors:
  $$F_b' = F_b \cdot C_D \cdot C_M \cdot C_t \cdot C_L \cdot C_F \cdot C_{fu} \cdot C_i \cdot C_r$$
  where $C_D$ is load duration factor, $C_M$ wet service, $C_L$ beam stability factor.
- **Mass Timber (CLT)**: Orthogonally stacked laminations providing two-way spanning slab performance with inherent fire resistance via surface charring ($0.65\text{ mm/min}$).

## Tools & Standards
- **Codes**: NDS (National Design Specification for Wood Construction), IBC.
- **Software**: WoodWorks Sizer/Shearwalls.
- **Canonical References**: Breyer et al. — *Design of Wood Structures-ASD/LRFD*.
