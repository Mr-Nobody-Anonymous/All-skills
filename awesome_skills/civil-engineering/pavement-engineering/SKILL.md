# Pavement Engineering

## Scope
Pavement engineering designs, constructs, and evaluates flexible (asphalt) and rigid (Portland cement concrete) pavement structures for highway and airport loadings.

## Design Frameworks
- **Equivalent Single Axle Load (ESAL)**: Standard $18,000\text{ lb}$ ($80\text{ kN}$) single axle equivalent.
- **Flexible Structural Number (SN, AASHTO 1993)**:
  $$\text{SN} = a_1 D_1 + a_2 D_2 m_2 + a_3 D_3 m_3$$
  where $a_i$ are layer structural coefficients, $D_i$ layer thicknesses, $m_i$ drainage coefficients.
- **Mechanistic-Empirical Pavement Design (MEPDG)**: Calculates tensile strain at bottom of asphalt (fatigue cracking) and vertical compressive strain at top of subgrade (rutting).

## Tools & Standards
- **Standards**: AASHTO Guide for Design of Pavement Structures, Superpave specifications (AASHTO M 320).
- **Software**: AASHTOWare Pavement ME Design, WinPAS.
