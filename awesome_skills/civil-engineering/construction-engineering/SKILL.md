# Construction Engineering

## Scope
Construction engineering coordinates the execution, sequencing, cost control, heavy equipment logistics, and site safety required to transform structural drawings into physical infrastructure.

## Management & Technical Systems
- **Critical Path Method (CPM)**: Early Start (ES), Early Finish (EF), Late Start (LS), Late Finish (LF); Total Float $\text{TF} = \text{LS} - \text{ES}$. Critical activities have $\text{TF} = 0$.
- **Earned Value Management (EVM)**:
  - Planned Value (PV), Earned Value (EV), Actual Cost (AC)
  - Cost Variance: $\text{CV} = \text{EV} - \text{AC}$; Schedule Variance: $\text{SV} = \text{EV} - \text{PV}$
  - Cost Performance Index: $\text{CPI} = \text{EV} / \text{AC}$; Schedule Performance Index: $\text{SPI} = \text{EV} / \text{PV}$
- **Temporary Structures**: Falsework, formwork pressure (ACI 347R), deep excavation shoring, trench boxes.

## Tools & Standards
- **Standards**: OSHA 29 CFR 1926 (Construction Safety), ACI 347R (Guide to Formwork).
- **Software**: Primavera P6, Procore, Bluebeam Revu, HeavyBid.
- **Canonical References**: Peurifoy et al. — *Construction Planning, Equipment, and Methods*.
