# Traffic Engineering

## Scope
Traffic engineering optimizes intersection operations, signalized corridors, roundabout geometry, and pedestrian safety systems.

## Formulations & Protocols
- **Webster's Optimal Cycle Length**:
  $$C_0 = \frac{1.5 L + 5}{1 - Y}$$
  where $L$ is total lost time per cycle, $Y = \sum y_i = \sum (q_i / s_i)$ is total flow ratio.
- **Delay Models (HCM 6th Edition)**: Uniform delay ($d_1$), incremental delay ($d_2$), residual delay ($d_3$).

## Tools & Standards
- **Standards**: MUTCD (Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices), HCM.
- **Software**: Synchro/SimTraffic, HCS (Highway Capacity Software), Vistro.
