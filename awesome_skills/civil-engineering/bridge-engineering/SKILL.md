# Bridge Engineering

## Scope
Bridge engineering focuses on the structural analysis, design, erection, maintenance, and load rating of highway and railway bridges.

## Design Codes & Formulations
- **AASHTO LRFD HL-93 Live Load**: Design truck (32-kip rear axles) + design lane load ($0.64\text{ kip/ft}$).
- **Dynamic Load Allowance (Impact, IM)**: Deck joints ($75\%$), all other components ($33\% \implies 1 + IM = 1.33$).
- **Prestressed Concrete Losses**: Immediate (elastic shortening) and time-dependent (creep, shrinkage, steel relaxation).
- **Bridge Load Rating**: Rating Factor $\text{RF} = \frac{C - \gamma_{DC} DC - \gamma_{DW} DW}{\gamma_{LL} (LL + IM)}$.

## Tools & Standards
- **Codes**: AASHTO LRFD Bridge Design Specifications, AREMA (Railway Bridges).
- **Software**: CSI Bridge, MIDAS Civil, LUSAS, LEAP Bridge.
- **Canonical References**: Barker & Puckett — *Design of Highway Bridges*.
