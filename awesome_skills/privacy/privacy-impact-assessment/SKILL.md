# DPIA and PIA Regulatory Risk Assessments

## Overview & Core Principles
Conduct formal Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIA) under Article 35 GDPR and state privacy frameworks.

Evaluate necessity, proportionality, and residual risk to rights and freedoms for high-risk data processing.
