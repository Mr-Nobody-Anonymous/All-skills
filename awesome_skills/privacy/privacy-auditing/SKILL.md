# Third-Party Vendor Privacy Auditing & Data Transfer Impact Assessments

## Overview & Core Principles
Audit vendor Data Processing Agreements (DPA), Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC), and Schrems II transfer risks.

Assess foreign surveillance laws and enforce supplementary technical measures (end-to-end encryption) for cross-border data.
