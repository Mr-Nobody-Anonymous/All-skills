# Masonry Trade & Structural Masonry

## Scope
Masonry construction, structural concrete masonry unit (CMU) walls, cavity walls, brick veneer, mortar mix selection, and seismic bond beams under TMS 402/602.

## Mortar Types & Structural Rules
- **ASTM C270 Mortar Proportioning (M, S, N, O - MaSoN wOrK)**:
  - Type M: High compressive strength ($2500\text{ psi}$); below-grade foundations, retaining walls.
  - Type S: High flexural bond strength ($1800\text{ psi}$); exterior at-grade seismic and wind walls.
  - Type N: General purpose medium strength ($750\text{ psi}$); exterior above-grade veneers.
  - Type O: Low strength ($350\text{ psi}$); interior non-load-bearing, historical restoration.
- **CMU Grouting & Reinforcement (TMS 402)**: Vertical rebar in hollow cells fully grouted with coarse grout; continuous horizontal bond beam rebar at floor and roof levels.

## Standards & Tools
- **Standards**: TMS 402/602 (Building Code Requirements and Specification for Masonry Structures).
- **Tools**: Mason's trowel, Story pole, Mason's line and blocks, Jointer.
