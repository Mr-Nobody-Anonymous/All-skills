# Flooring Trade & Installation

## Scope
Subfloor preparation, moisture vapor emission testing, ceramic/porcelain tile setting, hardwood nail-down, and resilient luxury vinyl plank (LVP) installations.

## Subfloor & Setting Standards
- **Subfloor Deflection (Tile / Stone, ANSI A108)**: Maximum allowable deflection under live load $L/360$ for ceramic tile; $L/720$ for natural stone.
- **Moisture Testing**: ASTM F1869 (Calcium Chloride test, $\le 3\text{ lbs}/1000\text{ sq ft}/24\text{ hr}$) and ASTM F2170 (In-situ Relative Humidity probes, $\le 75-80\%\text{ RH}$).
- **Tile Thinset Mortar (ANSI A118.4)**: Polymer-modified thinset; trowel notch sizing ($1/2 \times 1/2\text{ in}$ square notch for large-format tiles $>15\text{ in}$) with directional troweling and back-buttering ($>95\%$ mortar coverage).

## Standards & Tools
- **Standards**: TCNA Handbook for Ceramic, Glass, and Stone Tile Installation, NWFA.
