# Drywall & Wall Finishing

## Scope
Gypsum board hanging, joint compound finishing levels 1 through 5, corner reinforcement, fire-rated assemblies (Type X), and acoustic dampening installations.

## Finishing Levels (GA-214 / ASTM C840)
- **Level 1**: Tape embedded in joint compound; tool marks permissible (plenums, service corridors).
- **Level 2**: Tape embedded, one coat over fasteners and accessories (garages, warehouse).
- **Level 3**: Two coats over fasteners, one coat over tape; texture ready.
- **Level 4**: Three coats over fasteners, two over tape; standard residential finish for flat/egg-shell paint.
- **Level 5**: Level 4 plus a full skim coat applied over entire board surface to equalize porosity under gloss paints or severe critical lighting.
- **Fire-Rated Assemblies**: Type X ($5/8\text{ in}$) and Type C gypsum boards providing 1-hour to 2-hour fire-resistance ratings.

## Standards & Tools
- **Standards**: Gypsum Association GA-214, ASTM C840.
- **Tools**: Taping knives ($6, 10, 12\text{ in}$), Drywall mud pan, Automatic taping tools (Bazooka).
