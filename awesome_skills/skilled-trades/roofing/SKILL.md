# Roofing Trade & Waterproofing Systems

## Scope
Roofing installation, steep-slope shingle/tile/metal systems, low-slope commercial membranes (TPO/EPDM), step/counter flashing, and attic ventilation ratios.

## Technical Requirements & Codes
- **Attic Ventilation (IRC R806.1)**: $1:150$ net free ventilating area ratio ($1:300$ with vapor retarder and balanced $50/50$ soffit/ridge ventilation).
- **Steep-Slope Assemblies**: Drip edge at eaves/rakes, ice-and-water shield self-adhering membrane along eaves and valleys, underlayment (synthetic felt), architectural shingles with 4/6-nail fastening patterns.
- **Low-Slope Single-Ply Membranes**: TPO (heat-welded seams, $45-60\text{ mil}$), EPDM (tape-adhered seams), PVC (chemical resistance).

## Standards & Tools
- **Standards**: NRCA (National Roofing Contractors Association) Roofing Manual, ASTM D3462.
