# Commercial & Residential Painting

## Scope
Architectural coatings, substrate surface preparation (pressure washing, sanding, degreasing), primer binding chemistry, application techniques, and dry film thickness (DFT).

## Coatings Technology & Measurement
- **Surface Preparation Standards (SSPC / NACE)**: SP 1 (Solvent Cleaning), SP 2 (Hand Tool), SP 6 (Commercial Blast), SP 10 (Near-White Metal).
- **Spreading Rate & Film Thickness**:
  $$\text{WFT} = \frac{\text{DFT}}{\text{Volume Solids (fraction)}}, \quad \text{Coverage Area } (\text{sq ft/gal}) = \frac{1604 \times \text{Volume Solids}}{\text{DFT (mils)}}$$
- **Application Methods**: Airless spray ($2000-3000\text{ psi}$ for high-viscosity architectural coatings), HVLP (high volume low pressure for cabinetry/finishing), brush/roller.

## Standards & Tools
- **Standards**: SSPC (Society for Protective Coatings), Master Painters Institute (MPI).
- **Tools**: Wet Film Comb, Positector Magnetic DFT Gauge, Airless Sprayer.
