# Commercial Refrigeration Systems

## Scope
Installation, piping, and troubleshooting of commercial low and medium temperature refrigeration systems, walk-in coolers, supermarket rack systems, and defrost control.

## System Controls & Piping
- **Refrigerant Line Sizing**: Suction line sizing balancing pressure drop ($<2^\circ\text{F}$ saturation loss) with minimum refrigerant vapor velocity ($>1000\text{ FPM}$ horizontal, $>1500\text{ FPM}$ vertical) for oil return.
- **Defrost Systems**: Electric defrost heaters, hot-gas defrost bypass valves, defrost termination thermostats.
- **Pressure Regulators**: Evaporator Pressure Regulators (EPR) and Crankcase Pressure Regulators (CPR) preventing compressor motor overload.

## Standards & Tools
- **Standards**: ASHRAE 15 (Safety Standard for Refrigeration Systems), EPA 608.
