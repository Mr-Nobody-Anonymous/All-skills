# Automotive Service & Mechanical Repair

## Scope
Automotive maintenance and mechanical repairs covering disc/drum brake systems, steering/suspension alignment, drivetrain servicing, and OBD-II diagnostics.

## Mechanical & Alignment Systems
- **Wheel Alignment Angles**:
  - Camber: Inward/outward tilt of wheel top (affects tire shoulder wear).
  - Caster: Forward/rearward tilt of steering axis (affects straight-line stability).
  - Toe: Difference in distance between front and rear of tires on the same axle (dominant cause of tire scrubbing).
- **Hydraulic Braking**: Master cylinder, hydraulic pressure multiplication via Pascal's Law, ABS modulator valve operation, rotor runout limits ($<0.002\text{ in}$).

## Standards & Tools
- **Standards**: ASE (Automotive Service Excellence) Certification Standards.
- **Tools**: Bi-directional scan tool, Dial indicator, Torque wrench, Brake bleeder.
