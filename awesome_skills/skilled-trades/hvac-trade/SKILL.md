# HVAC Installation & Refrigeration Service

## Scope
Hands-on field installation, troubleshooting, vacuum evacuation, recovery, charging, and diagnostic testing of split air conditioning, heat pumps, and chillers.

## Field Diagnostics & Thermodynamic Charging
- **Superheat Calculation (Fixed Orifice / Cap Tube)**:
  $$\text{Superheat} = T_{\text{suction line}} - T_{\text{evap saturation (gauge)}}$$
- **Subcooling Calculation (Thermostatic Expansion Valve, TXV)**:
  $$\text{Subcooling} = T_{\text{cond saturation (gauge)}} - T_{\text{liquid line}}$$
- **Vacuum Evacuation**: Micron gauge pulling below $500\text{ microns}$, holding vacuum decay test (checks for moisture vs. non-condensable gas leak).
- **EPA Section 608 Compliance**: Recovery cylinders, leak rate repair thresholds, non-venting regulations for HFC/HFO refrigerants.

## Standards & Tools
- **Standards**: EPA Clean Air Act Section 608, AHRI Standard 210/240.
- **Tools**: Digital Manifold Gauges, Dual-stage Vacuum Pump, Recovery Machine, Electronic Micron Gauge.
