# Carpentry & Structural Framing

## Scope
Rough and finish carpentry, platform floor framing, load-bearing stud walls, structural roof rafters/trusses, and precision architectural millwork.

## Framing Rules & Engineering
- **Platform Framing Layout**: Studs, joists, and rafters laid out on standard $16\text{ in}$ or $24\text{ in}$ on-center (OC) spacing.
- **Roof Rafter Geometry**: Rise, run, pitch ($X/12$). Rafter length: $L = \sqrt{\text{run}^2 + \text{rise}^2} - \frac{1}{2}\text{ridge thickness} + \text{overhang}$.
- **Structural Headers**: Sized based on opening span and building storeys (e.g., double $2\times 10$ with $1/2\text{ in}$ plywood spacer for $6\text{ ft}$ exterior wall opening).
- **Shear Wall Nailing (IRC R602.10)**: Fastener spacing ($6\text{ in}$ edges, $12\text{ in}$ field) with structural wood panels (OSB/Plywood) for lateral wind/seismic shear resistance.

## Standards & Tools
- **Standards**: International Residential Code (IRC), Western Wood Products Association (WWPA).
- **Tools**: Speed square, Framing hammer, Circular saw, Pneumatic framing nailer, Laser level.
