# Lean Startup Customer Discovery and Problem Validation

## Overview & Core Principles
Design and conduct non-leading customer discovery interviews using The Mom Test principles to surface genuine willingness-to-pay and validated pain points.

### Discovery Interview Protocol (The Mom Test Standard)
1. **Core Operating Constraints**:
   - Never pitch the solution or ask "Would you buy a product that does X?".
   - Ask exclusively about concrete past behaviors: "When was the last time you encountered X? What did you do? What did it cost you?".
   - Look for actively hacked workarounds (spreadsheets, manual scripts, dedicated contractor hours) as proof of acute budgetable pain.
2. **Five Essential Discovery Questions**:
   - What is the hardest part about [doing business process]?
   - When was the last time you encountered that problem? Walk me through what happened.
   - Why was that hard? What was the downstream consequence?
   - What, if anything, have you done to solve this problem?
   - What don't you love about the solutions you've already tried?
