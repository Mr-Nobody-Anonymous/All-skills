# Skill: Procurement Category Strategy

## Purpose

Create a structured procurement category strategy that can be used for management alignment, sourcing roadmap and supplier portfolio decisions.

## When to use

Use this skill when the user asks about:

- category strategy,
- supplier mix,
- sourcing roadmap,
- spend category review,
- risk reduction,
- sourcing strategy,
- future procurement model,
- transition to a new employer or ownership structure.

## Required inputs

If available:

- category name,
- annual spend,
- main suppliers,
- business criticality,
- technical complexity,
- supply risks,
- price trends,
- internal stakeholders,
- current contract coverage,
- quality / delivery KPIs,
- strategic objectives.

## Process

1. Define category scope.
2. Map spend and subcategories.
3. Map current suppliers.
4. Assess business criticality.
5. Assess market and supplier risks.
6. Evaluate current supplier mix.
7. Identify quick wins.
8. Define strategic options.
9. Recommend target supplier portfolio.
10. Create implementation roadmap.

## Output format

```markdown
## 1. Kategorie a rozsah
## 2. Současný stav
## 3. Dodavatelská mapa
## 4. Rizika
## 5. Příležitosti
## 6. Doporučená strategie
## 7. Roadmapa
## 8. Rozhodnutí pro management
```

## Rules

- Do not assume that single-sourcing is bad in every case.
- Do not assume that more suppliers always means lower risk.
- Consider business continuity, quality, complexity and negotiation leverage.
- Make recommendations actionable.
