# Skill: Management Procurement Reporting

## Purpose

Create clear, concise and decision-ready procurement reports for management.

## When to use

Use this skill when the user needs:

- monthly procurement report,
- board summary,
- executive summary,
- savings report,
- risk overview,
- supplier status,
- procurement transition update,
- management presentation text.

## Required inputs

If available:

- reporting period,
- audience,
- key procurement activities,
- savings vs baseline,
- open risks,
- supplier issues,
- major RFQs,
- contract updates,
- compliance status,
- required decisions.

## Process

1. Identify audience and reporting purpose.
2. Summarize key message first.
3. Separate financial impact, risks and actions.
4. Highlight decisions needed from management.
5. Keep details in appendices or tables.
6. Use concise business language.
7. Avoid operational noise unless it affects risk or financial impact.

## Output format

```markdown
## Procurement Management Summary

### 1. Hlavní sdělení
### 2. Finanční dopad
### 3. Klíčové aktivity
### 4. Rizika
### 5. Rozhodnutí / podpora potřebná od managementu
### 6. Další kroky
```

## Rules

- Management reports must be concise.
- Always lead with the main message.
- Avoid too many operational details.
- Every risk should have an owner or next step if possible.
