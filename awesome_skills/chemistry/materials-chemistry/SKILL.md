# Materials Chemistry

## Scope
Materials chemistry focuses on the design, synthesis, and characterization of solid-state structures with targeted electronic, magnetic, optical, catalytic, and mechanical functions.

## Solid-State Synthesis & Characterization

### 1. Synthetic Methodologies
- **Solid-State Ceramic Route**: High temperature diffusion between powdered precursors; governed by Fick's laws of diffusion.
- **Sol-Gel Processing**: Hydrolysis and condensation of metal alkoxides $M(OR)_n \to$ colloidal sol $\to$ interconnected gel $\to$ drying (xerogel/aerogel) $\to$ calcination.
- **Hydrothermal & Solvothermal**: High-pressure, subcritical/supercritical solvent crystallization in autoclaves.
- **Chemical Vapor Deposition (CVD)** & **Atomic Layer Deposition (ALD)**: Thin film growth with monolayer precision.

### 2. Defect Chemistry (Kröger-Vink Notation)
- Vacancies ($V_M^{\prime\prime}$), interstitials ($M_i^{\bullet\bullet}$), electronic defects ($e^\prime, h^\bullet$).
- Schottky defect: Anion + cation vacancy pair (maintains stoichiometry).
- Frenkel defect: Ion displaced into interstitial site.
