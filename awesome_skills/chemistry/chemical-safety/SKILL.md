# Chemical Safety

## Scope
Chemical safety provides protocols for recognizing, evaluating, and controlling chemical hazards across laboratories and industrial facilities to prevent exposure, toxic releases, fires, and explosions.

## Safety Frameworks & Regulations

### 1. Globally Harmonized System (GHS) & SDS
- **16-Section Safety Data Sheet (SDS)**: Section 2 (Hazards Identification), Section 4 (First Aid), Section 8 (Exposure Controls/PPE), Section 10 (Stability and Reactivity).
- **H-Statements (Hazard)**: H200s (Physical), H300s (Health), H400s (Environmental).
- **P-Statements (Precautionary)**: P100s (General), P200s (Prevention), P300s (Response).

### 2. Chemical Incompatibility & Storage
- Segregate oxidizers from organics/reducing agents.
- Separate strong acids (nitric, sulfuric) from bases ($NaOH$) and cyanide/sulfide salts.
- Store flammable solvents ($Flash point < 60^\circ\text{C}$) in dedicated NFPA fire-rated cabinets.
- Peroxide formers (ethers, THF): Test regularly with peroxide strips.

## Standards & References
- **Standards**: OSHA 29 CFR 1910.1450 (Lab Standard), NFPA 45 (Standard on Fire Protection for Laboratories Using Chemicals).
- **Canonical References**: Prudent Practices in the Laboratory (National Academies Press).
