# Nanochemistry

## Scope
Nanochemistry investigates chemical synthesis and surface phenomena at the nanoscale ($1 - 100\text{ nm}$), where quantum size effects and high surface-area-to-volume ratios dominate behavior.

## Core Nanoscale Phenomena

### 1. Quantum Confinement & Surface Energy
- **Bohr Exciton Radius**: Size at which quantum confinement emerges.
  - Brus Equation (quantum dot bandgap shift):
    $$\Delta E_g(r) = \frac{\hbar^2 \pi^2}{2 r^2}\left(\frac{1}{m_e^*} + \frac{1}{m_h^*}\right) - \frac{1.8 e^2}{4\pi\varepsilon r}$$
- **Surface-to-Volume Ratio**: Fraction of surface atoms $F_s \propto 1/r$; drives melting point depression and elevated catalytic reactivity.

### 2. Nanomaterial Characterization
- **Dynamic Light Scattering (DLS)**: Stokes-Einstein equation for hydrodynamic diameter:
  $$d_H = \frac{k_B T}{3\pi \eta D}$$
- **Electron Microscopy**: Transmission Electron Microscopy (TEM) and Scanning TEM (STEM-HAADF) for lattice fringe spacing and morphology.

## Standards & References
- **Standards**: ISO/TS 80004 (Nanotechnologies vocabulary), ASTM E2490 (DLS).
