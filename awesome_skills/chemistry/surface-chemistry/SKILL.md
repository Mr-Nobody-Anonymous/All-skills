# Surface Chemistry

## Scope
Surface chemistry studies physical and chemical phenomena occurring at interfaces (solid-liquid, solid-gas, liquid-gas), including surface tension, wetting, adsorption, and colloidal stability.

## Interfacial Physics & Formulations

### 1. Surface Tension & Wetting
- **Young's Equation**:
  $$\gamma_{SG} = \gamma_{SL} + \gamma_{LG} \cos \theta_c$$
  where $\theta_c$ is contact angle ($\theta < 90^\circ$ hydrophilic/wetting, $\theta > 90^\circ$ hydrophobic).
- **Young-Laplace Equation**: Pressure difference across curved interface: $\Delta P = \gamma (1/R_1 + 1/R_2)$.

### 2. BET Surface Area Determination
- **Brunauer-Emmett-Teller (BET) Equation**:
  $$\frac{P/P_0}{V (1 - P/P_0)} = \frac{1}{V_m C} + \frac{C - 1}{V_m C}\left(\frac{P}{P_0}\right)$$
  Yields specific surface area ($S_{\text{BET}}$, $m^2/g$) from $N_2$ physisorption at $77\text{ K}$.

## Standards & References
- **Standards**: ISO 9277 (BET surface area of solids), ASTM D1331 (Surface tension).
