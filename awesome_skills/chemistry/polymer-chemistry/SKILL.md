# Polymer Chemistry

## Scope
Polymer chemistry examines the synthesis, kinetics, characterization, and physical properties of synthetic and natural macromolecules.

## Polymerization Kinetics & Physics

### 1. Molecular Weight Distributions
- **Number-Average Molecular Weight**: $\bar{M}_n = \sum N_i M_i / \sum N_i$.
- **Weight-Average Molecular Weight**: $\bar{M}_w = \sum N_i M_i^2 / \sum N_i M_i$.
- **Dispersity (PDI)**: $Đ = \bar{M}_w / \bar{M}_n \ge 1.0$.

### 2. Polymerization Mechanisms
- **Step-Growth (Carothers Equation)**:
  $$\bar{X}_n = \frac{1}{1 - p}$$
  High molecular weight requires very high conversion $p > 0.99$.
- **Chain-Growth (Free Radical, Anionic, Cationic, RAFT, ATRP)**:
  Initiation, propagation ($k_p$), termination ($k_t$), chain transfer ($k_{tr}$).

### 3. Thermal & Mechanical Behavior
- **Glass Transition ($T_g$)**: Amorphous segment mobility; Fox equation for copolymers:
  $$\frac{1}{T_g} = \frac{w_1}{T_{g1}} + \frac{w_2}{T_{g2}}$$
- **Melting Temperature ($T_m$)**: Crystalline domain dissolution.
- **Gel Permeation Chromatography (GPC/SEC)**: Measures hydrodynamic volume calibrated against polystyrene standards.

## Standards & References
- **Standards**: ASTM D638 (Tensile properties), ASTM D3418 (DSC thermal properties).
- **Canonical References**: Odian — *Principles of Polymerization*; Flory — *Principles of Polymer Chemistry*.
