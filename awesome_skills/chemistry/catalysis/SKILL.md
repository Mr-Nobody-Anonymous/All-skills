# Catalysis

## Scope
Catalysis investigates substances that increase the rate of a chemical transformation without being consumed, lowering activation energy ($E_a$) by stabilizing reaction intermediates and transition states.

## Catalytic Mechanisms & Quantification

### 1. Metrics of Catalytic Performance
- **Turnover Number (TON)**: $\text{TON} = \text{moles of product} / \text{moles of catalyst}$.
- **Turnover Frequency (TOF)**: $\text{TOF} = \text{TON} / \text{time } (\text{s}^{-1})$.
- **Selectivity**: $\text{Selectivity}_i = \text{Yield}_i / \sum \text{Yield}_j \times 100\%$.

### 2. Surface Adsorption Models (Heterogeneous)
- **Langmuir Adsorption Isotherm**:
  $$\theta = \frac{K P}{1 + K P}$$
  where $\theta$ is fractional surface coverage, $K$ is adsorption equilibrium constant.
- **Langmuir-Hinshelwood Mechanism**: Reaction occurs between two chemisorbed species: $\text{Rate} = k \theta_A \theta_B$.
- **Eley-Rideal Mechanism**: Gas-phase molecule reacts directly with chemisorbed species: $\text{Rate} = k P_A \theta_B$.
