# AP Style Press Release Formulation & Distribution

## Overview & Core Principles
Author newswire-ready press releases conforming to Associated Press (AP) style with compelling headlines and executive quotes.

Structure press releases with the inverted pyramid: Lead, Essential Facts, Supporting Quotes, Boilerplate, Media Contact.
