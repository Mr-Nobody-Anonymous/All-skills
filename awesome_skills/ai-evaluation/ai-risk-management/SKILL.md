# NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF)

## Scope
Implementation of the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0) to identify, analyze, assess, and manage risks associated with artificial intelligence systems.

## The Four Core Functions
1. **GOVERN**: Cultivate a culture of risk management; establish organizational roles, accountability, and AI ethical policies.
2. **MAP**: Contextualize AI risks; document intended purpose, data dependencies, stakeholder impacts, and deployment environments.
3. **MEASURE**: Quantify AI trustworthiness characteristics using objective benchmarks, human evaluations, and ongoing tracking.
4. **MANAGE**: Prioritize and allocate resources to mitigate identified risks, implement safeguards, and monitor operational performance.

## Trustworthy AI Characteristics
- Valid & Reliable, Safe, Secure & Resilient, Accountable & Transparent, Explainable & Interpretable, Privacy-Preserving, Fair with Harmful Bias Managed.

## Standards & References
- **Standards**: NIST AI RMF 1.0 (NIST SP 1270), ISO/IEC 42001 (Artificial Intelligence Management System).
