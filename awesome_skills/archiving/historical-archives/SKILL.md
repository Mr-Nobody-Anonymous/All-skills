# Historical Physical Archives & Conservation Management

## Overview & Core Principles
Maintain physical manuscript archives adhering to archival climate control, acid-free rehousing, and disaster salvage.

Enforce 65°F temperature / 45% RH relative humidity storage parameters to prevent chemical degradation.
