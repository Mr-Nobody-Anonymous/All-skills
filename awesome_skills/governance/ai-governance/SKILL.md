# AI Governance & NIST AI Risk Management Framework

## Overview & Core Principles
Operationalize trustworthy AI systems adhering to EU AI Act risk tiers and NIST AI RMF 1.0.

Classify AI models into prohibited, high, and minimal risk; mandate bias testing and human-in-the-loop oversight.
