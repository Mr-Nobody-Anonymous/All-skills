# SR 11-7 Model Risk Management and Validation

## Overview & Core Principles
Validate algorithmic trading, credit underwriting, and risk models conforming to Federal Reserve SR 11-7.

Maintain independent model validation pipelines testing conceptual soundness, ongoing monitoring, and outcome analysis.
