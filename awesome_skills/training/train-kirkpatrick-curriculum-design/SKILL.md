# Enterprise Training Curriculum Design & Kirkpatrick Level 4 Evaluation

## Overview & Core Principles
Develop corporate technical training programs and measure business impact across reaction, learning, behavior transfer, and organizational ROI.

### Kirkpatrick 4-Level Evaluation Framework
1. **Level 1: Reaction**: Measure participant engagement, perceived relevance, and net promoter score (post-session survey).
2. **Level 2: Learning**: Pre- and post-training objective capability assessments (practical coding katas, rubric-scored presentations).
3. **Level 3: Behavior**: 90-day post-training behavioral audits observing workplace application (peer reviews, pull request quality, process compliance).
4. **Level 4: Results (Business ROI)**:
   - Quantifiable operational metric shift: Mean Time to Remediation (MTTR) reduction, defect escape rate reduction, or sales cycle compression attributable to training intervention.
5. **Instructional Architecture**:
   - Microlearning modules bounded to 7-12 minutes.
   - 70-20-10 Learning Model: 70% job-embedded practice, 20% mentorship and peer coaching, 10% formal coursework.
