# Socratic Pedagogy and Worked-Example Fading

## Overview & Core Principles
Design instructional learning dialogues using cognitive apprenticeship, Socratic diagnostic questioning, and faded worked-example progressions.

### Cognitive Apprenticeship Architecture
1. **Worked-Example Effect**:
   - Novice learners acquire problem-solving schemas much faster through studying complete step-by-step solutions than through unguided problem-solving.
2. **Backward Fading Progression**:
   - Step 1: Complete worked example (Steps 1, 2, 3 fully solved and annotated).
   - Step 2: Faded worked example (Steps 1 and 2 solved, learner completes Step 3).
   - Step 3: Deeply faded worked example (Step 1 solved, learner completes Steps 2 and 3).
   - Step 4: Independent problem solving with automated prompt for self-explanation.
3. **Socratic Diagnostic Protocol**:
   - Never provide the correct answer immediately when an error is detected.
   - Highlight the contradiction in the learner's mental model: "What does this equation predict if temperature drops to zero? How does that compare to what you observed?".
