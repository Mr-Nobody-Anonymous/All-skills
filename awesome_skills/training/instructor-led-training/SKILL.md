# Instructor-Led Training (ILT) Facilitation & Classroom Engagement

## Overview & Core Principles
Facilitate interactive adult learning sessions using breakout activities, live polling, and discussion management.

Apply Knowles's Principles of Andragogy (adult learners are self-directed, relevancy-oriented, and problem-centered).
