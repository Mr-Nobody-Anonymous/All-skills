# Lifecycle Retargeting and Frequency Capping

## Overview & Core Principles
Architect cross-channel retargeting funnels with strict frequency caps and recency tiering.

Segment audience windows: 0-3 days (high intent), 4-14 days (objection handling), 15-30 days (discount/promo).
