# Corporate Taxonomy Design and Controlled Vocabularies

## Overview & Core Principles
Construct hierarchical, faceted taxonomies and controlled vocabularies adhering to ISO 25964 standards.

Maintain preferred terms, non-preferred synonyms (USE/UF), broader terms (BT), and narrower terms (NT).
