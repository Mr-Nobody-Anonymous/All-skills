# QALY and DALY Methodological Framework

## 1. Quality-Adjusted Life Years (QALY)

A QALY combines both the length (quantity) and quality of life generated by a health medical intervention.

### Mathematical Formulation
$$\text{QALY} = \sum_{t=1}^{T} Q_t \times L_t \times \frac{1}{(1 + r)^t}$$

Where:
- $Q_t$: Health-related quality of life (HRQoL) utility score between $0.0$ (death) and $1.0$ (perfect health). States worse than death can yield negative scores (e.g. $-0.2$).
- $L_t$: Length of time spent in that health state (in years).
- $r$: Discount rate.

### Multi-Attribute Utility Instruments (MAUI)
- **EQ-5D (EuroQol)**: Evaluates 5 dimensions (Mobility, Self-Care, Usual Activities, Pain/Discomfort, Anxiety/Depression) on 3-level (EQ-5D-3L) or 5-level (EQ-5D-5L) scales, mapped to country tariffs.
- **SF-6D**: Derived from the SF-36 health survey.
- **HUI (Health Utilities Index)**: HUI2 and HUI3 scoring systems.

## 2. Disability-Adjusted Life Years (DALY)

Widely used in global health, WHO burden of disease studies, and lower/middle income country assessments.

### Mathematical Formulation
$$\text{DALY} = \text{YLL} + \text{YLD}$$

1. **Years of Life Lost (YLL)**:
   $$\text{YLL} = N \times L$$
   Where $N$ is number of deaths and $L$ is standard life expectancy at age of death.

2. **Years Lost due to Disability (YLD)**:
   $$\text{YLD} = I \times DW \times D$$
   Where $I$ is incident cases, $DW$ is disability weight (from 0.0 perfect health to 1.0 death), and $D$ is average duration of the condition.

---

## 3. Comparison Summary

| Metric | Goal | Scale Direction | Common Setting |
|--------|------|-----------------|----------------|
| **QALY** | Measure health *gained* | Higher is better ($1 = \text{full health}$) | HTA, High-income country reimbursement (NICE, CADTH) |
| **DALY** | Measure disease *burden* | Lower is better ($0 = \text{no burden}$) | Global burden of disease, WHO evaluations, LMIC policy |
