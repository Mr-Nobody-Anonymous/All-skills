# Guidelines for Cost-Effectiveness and Cost-Utility Analyses (CHEERS 2022)

## 1. Core Economic Evaluation Types

1. **Cost-Minimization Analysis (CMA)**: Used *only* when clinical outcomes are proven identical. Compares costs exclusively.
2. **Cost-Effectiveness Analysis (CEA)**: Outcomes measured in natural clinical units (e.g. cost per life-year gained, cost per mmHg blood pressure reduction).
3. **Cost-Utility Analysis (CUA)**: Outcomes measured in generic preference-weighted units (QALYs or DALYs). Allows cross-disease comparisons.
4. **Cost-Benefit Analysis (CBA)**: Both costs and outcomes converted into monetary currency ($). Uses Net Present Value (NPV) or Benefit-Cost Ratios.

---

## 2. CHEERS 2022 Checklist Summary

When generating or reviewing an economic evaluation report, verify adherence to the 28 CHEERS (Consolidated Health Economic Evaluation Reporting Standards) items:

1. **Title**: Identifies the intervention, comparator, and study type.
2. **Abstract**: Structured summary of background, methods, results, and conclusion.
3. **Background & Objective**: Clear policy question and clinical rationale.
4. **Target Population**: Clinical characteristics, subgroups, and demographics.
5. **Setting & Location**: Healthcare delivery system context.
6. **Study Perspective**: Explicitly stated (Payer, Health System, or Societal).
7. **Comparators**: Standard of care or real-world alternative.
8. **Time Horizon**: Sufficiently long to capture all differential costs and outcomes (often lifetime).
9. **Discount Rate**: Applied to both costs and benefits (explicitly justified if different).
10. **Choice of Health Outcomes**: Detailed rationale for primary outcome measure.
11. **Measurement of Outcomes**: Source of utility or clinical parameters (RCTs, meta-analysis).
12. **Valuation of Outcomes**: Tariffs or weighting sets used.
13. **Cost Estimation**: Unit costs, resource utilization, and cost years (adjusted for inflation).
14. **Currency and Conversion**: Base year specified and exchange rates if multi-country.
15. **Decision Model**: Mathematical structure (Markov, discrete event simulation) depicted transparently.
16. **Assumptions**: Explicit documentation of structural assumptions.
17. **Analytical Methods**: Half-cycle correction, convergence, probabilistic sensitivity parameters.
18. **Study Results**: Base-case incremental costs, incremental QALYs, and ICERs.
19. **Uncertainty Analysis**: Both deterministic (tornado) and probabilistic (CEAC curves) presented.
20. **Discussion**: Limitations, generalizability, and policy implications.
